\section{Porting a Paper from Another Template}
\label{app:porting}

A paper written for the Meta FAIR, ServiceNow or ByteDance Seed class compiles here after its \verb|\documentclass| line changes to \texttt{banglab}.
The commands those classes define, including \verb|\checkdata|, \verb|\morelinks|, \verb|\beginappendix| and \verb|\nm|, keep their meaning.
Author marks may be written as text or as maths symbols, since \verb|\dagger| and \verb|\ddagger| are mapped to their text forms inside the marks.

\section{Brand Assets}
\label{app:brand-assets}

The logos in \texttt{brand/} are vector PDFs converted from the SVG files served by \url{https://mila.quebec}.
The Mila wordmark keeps its own purple, \texttt{\#662E7D}, and the UdeM signature is set in UdeM blue, \texttt{\#0057AC}, the colour \url{https://www.umontreal.ca} uses.
